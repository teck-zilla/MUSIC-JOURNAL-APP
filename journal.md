MUSIC JOURNAL APP

This document shows the stage-by-stage process of what I worked on,  the structural/architechtural decisions I made
and challenges encountered and how I solved them as I progressed.

Stage 1: Planning and Tech Requirements
Mission parameter
Goals: Core requirements for a functional music journal app
Resources:
HTML5, Vanilla CSS3 (with CSS Custom Properties), and ES6+ Vanilla JavaScript.



To make the app accessible and available to all, I had to enable user login and profile authentication across four major streaming platforms: Spotify, Apple Music, Audiomack, and SoundCloud.
